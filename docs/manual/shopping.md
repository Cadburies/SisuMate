# Shopping & Spares — user guide

_Generated from the Sisu Mate feature map (`home/shopping`)._

## Shopping & Spares

Your boat's shopping and spares list, grouped by category.

**Where:** "Shopping & Spares" title bar over a sectioned list, with a round + button bottom-right.

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.

**What it does:** Shows the shopping list; tap an item for details or swipe it to change its state.

### Add shopping item

**Pro feature.** 
Adds one custom item to the Shopping list (saved under Misc). Pro only.

**Where:** Round "+" button, bottom-right of the Shopping & Spares screen.

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Add item** button.

**What it does:** Opens "Add Shopping Item" where you enter the item name (required), quantity, origin, notes and last price paid.

### Buy before passage

**Pro feature.** 
A banner listing the boat-critical items still to buy before you sail, with a shortcut to sort by priority.

**Where:** Tinted card at the top of Shopping titled "Buy before passage" with a "Sort by priority" button.

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Add item** button.
4. Enter **Impeller** in **Item Name**.
5. Tap **Add Item**.

**What it does:** Lists pending boat-critical items; "Sort by priority" switches the list to priority order.

### Customs check

Check items you are carrying (drones, spearguns, fresh meat, spirits) against an offline customs reminder pack, or ask AI about a pasted excerpt.

**Where:** Passport-style button in the Shopping title bar; opens "Customs check".

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Customs check (list)** button.

**What it does:** "Check offline pack" works with no internet; the AI option needs a connection and a key. Not an official customs determination.

### Shopping item page

**Pro feature.** 
Full page for one shopping item: quantity, price, status, origin and when it was last changed.

**Where:** Item name, "&lt;category&gt; · N of M", quantity, price, status, with Bought, Hide and Edit buttons.

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Add item** button.
4. Enter **Impeller** in **Item Name**.
5. Tap **Add Item**.
6. Tap **Spares**.
7. Tap **Impeller**.

**What it does:** Mark it bought, hide it or edit it; swipe sideways for the next item.

### Mark an item bought

**Pro feature.** 
Swipe a shopping item left to mark it bought; it drops out of "to buy".

**Where:** Swiping an item row left reveals "Complete".

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Add item** button.
4. Enter **Impeller** in **Item Name**.
5. Tap **Add Item**.
6. Tap **Spares**.
7. Swipe **Impeller** and tap **Complete**.

**What it does:** Marks the item bought; the trip estimate updates.

### Shopping menu

Shopping options: show hidden items, email all lists, complete the shopping run, plus the shared main menu.

**Where:** Menu from the Shopping title bar titled "Filters & Options".

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Menu** button.

**What it does:** Opens the Shopping menu.

#### Complete the shopping run

Clears everything you marked bought so the list is ready for the next trip; items still to buy stay.

**Where:** "Complete shopping run" in the Shopping menu; asks "Complete shopping run?".

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Menu** button.
4. Tap **Complete shopping run**.

**What it does:** "Clear bought" removes bought items; pantry and bar stock is not changed.

#### Email all shopping lists

Sends every shopping section in one email from your phone's mail app.

**Where:** "Email All Lists" in the Shopping menu.

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Menu** button.
4. Tap **Email All Lists**.

**What it does:** Opens your mail app with the whole list written out.

### Plan a port run

Plans a shopping trip ashore: what to buy, which kinds of shop, and local tips, working offline with live shop names optional.

**Where:** Map-pin button in the Shopping title bar; opens "Port run · N to buy".

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Plan port run** button.

**What it does:** Groups the to-buy list by shop type; set the port or allow location to name real shops; Copy shares the plan.

### Item shop guide

**Pro feature.** 
Offline advice on where to buy one item and any customs flags, without needing a connection.

**Where:** Small purple sparkle badge on each item row.

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap the **Add item** button.
4. Enter **Impeller** in **Item Name**.
5. Tap **Add Item**.
6. Tap **Spares**.
7. Tap the **Shop guide: Impeller** button.

**What it does:** Opens "Shop: &lt;item&gt;" with the kinds of shop to try and a compliance reminder; "Plan port run for this list" jumps to the port run.

### Sort the shopping list

Choose how the shopping list is ordered: original, name, what is needed first, or price.

**Where:** Row of chips under the title bar: Original, Name A-Z, Needed First, Price Low-High.

1. Open Sisu Mate on the Home screen.
2. Tap **Shopping**.
3. Tap **Name A-Z**.

**What it does:** Re-orders every category by the chosen chip.
