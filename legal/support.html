Sisu Mate Support

Sisu Mate is an offline-first app for running a boat: checklists, maintenance, log, weather, provisioning, crew, and games.

Email: sailingsisu@outlook.com

Video tips: search YouTube for “Sailing Sisu”.

Common questions

Does it work without signal? Yes. Everything important is on the device. Pro syncs when you are back online.

How do I get Pro? Open Upgrade in the drawer and buy monthly or yearly. Use Restore Purchases if you already paid on another phone.

How does crew join? The owner shares the boat code. Crew enter it under Join a boat. They do not need their own Pro subscription.

How do I delete my data? Uninstall or factory-reset for the phone copy. Email us from your account address to delete the cloud account.

Permissions? Location, camera, photos, and local network are optional and only used when you tap the matching feature.
