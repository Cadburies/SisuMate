Lists: Open a list and work the tiles. Colour is the status — no tick or cart icon.

How to use
• Tap a tile to open, edit, save.
• Swipe left = done / in stock.
• Swipe right = hide, shopping, or delete.
• Hidden stays in the list until you unhide it.

Colours

┌───────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Colour    │ Meaning                                                 │
├───────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Grey      │ Still to do, or listed but not aboard. No alarm.        │
├───────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Green     │ Done / aboard.                                          │
├───────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Blue      │ On the shopping list.                                   │
├───────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Red       │ Missing for this recipe (Chef / Cocktails only). Alarm. │
├───────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Dark grey │ Hidden on purpose.                                      │
└───────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Grey on pantry/bar means “not aboard.” Red only means “this dish or drink is blocked.” They are not two names for the same thing.
