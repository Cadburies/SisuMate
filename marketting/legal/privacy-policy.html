Sisu Mate Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026

Sisu Mate is published by Frik Olivier (Sailing Sisu). Contact: sailingsisu@outlook.com.

What this app is

Sisu Mate is an offline-first boat operations app. Checklists, logs, inventory, recipes, and games work on the device with no account. An optional account unlocks Pro sync and crew sharing.

Data we collect

We only collect the items below if you use the matching feature.

  • Account. Email and password or magic-link if you create a boat account.
  • Boat content you type. Checklists, maintenance, safety, captain’s log (including GPS if you stamp a position), fuel, inventory, documents, shopping, recipes, cocktails, polar samples, anchor spots.
  • Crew details you type. Name, role, phone, email, ICE contact, date of birth, nationality, passport number, dietary and allergen tags. These exist so you can produce port paperwork and meal plans. They sync with the boat if sync is on.
  • Location if you allow it: weather, log position, anchor alarm, port-run shopping, nearby-parts lookup.
  • Purchases. Pro subscription status via Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. We do not see your full card number.
  • Advertising identifiers on the Free tier, used by Google AdMob to show ads. Pro hides ads.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos you attach to checklist items, log entries, or pantry/bar items are saved to your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers, even with sync on.
  • The on-device error log is never uploaded.
  • LLM API keys you paste are stored on the boat record you control and are sent only to the provider you chose.
  • Instrument-gateway passwords (PredictWind Hub, YDWG, Home Assistant) stay on the device.
  • Guest profiles used in the galley are local-only.

Who else sees data

  • Supabase — our database and auth for accounts and Pro/crew sync.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play / RevenueCat — to sell and restore Pro.
  • Google AdMob — ads on the Free tier (device identifiers; may use approximate location if the OS allows).
  • Weather and map providers — when you load a forecast or chart, they receive a position or IP to return tiles and weather.
  • Crew on your boat — people who join with your share code can sync the boat’s content, including crew fields you entered.

We do not sell your data.

Children

Sisu Mate is not directed at children under 13. Cocktail recipes are for adults.

Your choices

  • Deny location, camera, photos, or local-network permission. Those features just stay off.
  • Stay signed out. The app still works offline.
  • Uninstall or use factory reset to wipe the local database.
  • Email sailingsisu@outlook.com from your account address to delete the cloud account and synced boat data.

Retention

Local data lives until you delete it or uninstall. Cloud boat data lives while the account is open, or until you ask us to delete it. Ads and store purchases follow Google, Apple, and RevenueCat retention rules.

Changes

We will update this page if the data we collect changes. The effective date at the top is the latest version.
