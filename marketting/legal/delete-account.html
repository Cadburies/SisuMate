Delete Your Sisu Mate Account

Sisu Mate, published by Sailing Sisu (developer: Frik Olivier), lets you request permanent deletion of your account and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — delete it yourself, in the app (instant)

  1. Open Sisu Mate and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete My Account (shown in red).
  3. Confirm on the warning dialog. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Your account and the cloud data described below are deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2 — request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to sailingsisu@outlook.com from the same email address registered on your account — for example if you're signed out and can't get back in.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Sisu Mate account".
  3. We verify the request and delete your account and associated cloud data, normally within 30 days.
  4. You'll receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

If you only want the app's local copy removed from your device, you can instead uninstall the app, or use Settings → Factory Reset inside the app — no request needed for that, and it doesn't touch your cloud account.
