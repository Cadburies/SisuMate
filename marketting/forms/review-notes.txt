Sisu Mate is an offline-first boat operations app. Most features work with no account and no network.

Sign-in is optional. Free users can browse seeded checklists, recipes, and games. Creating an account is only needed to test Pro sync and crew share codes.

In-app purchases:
• Sisu Mate Pro monthly — sisu_mate_pro_monthly (iOS) / promonthly:promonthly (Android)
• Sisu Mate Pro yearly — sisu_mate_pro_yearly (iOS) / proyearly:yearly (Android)
Restore Purchases is on the paywall.

Permissions are optional and used only when the user taps the matching feature:
• Location — centre weather, stamp a log position, anchor alarm, port-run shopping, nearby-parts lookup
• Camera / photos — barcodes and item photos
• Local network — LAN multiplayer games on the boat Wi-Fi

Ads (banner, native, interstitial) show on the Free tier only. Pro hides ads.

No demo password is required. Create a new email on the Boat account screen if you want to test sync.
